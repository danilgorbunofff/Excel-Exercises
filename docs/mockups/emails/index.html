Rules every email follows

  • Rendered from src/server/mail-templates.ts, the code that sends them. /api/cron/daily/ sends one email a day at 12:00 UTC: welcome first, the series 2, 4, 7, 10 and 14 days after it, the daily exercise on other days. PRO members get no selling emails.
  • One headline, one or two lines of text, one block (sheet, bars, links or list), one green button.
  • Starter emails end with today’s exercise in two lines. Only the daily email shows the sheet.
  • Tables, inline CSS, 600px, system fonts. Dark mode via color-scheme and prefers-color-scheme.
  • Footer: why you get it, one-click unsubscribe (plus a List-Unsubscribe header when sent), weekly instead.
