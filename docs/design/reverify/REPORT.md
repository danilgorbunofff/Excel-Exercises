## 1. The reference in five lines

1. **Does well:** a calm Excel-like workbench (instructions left, green-barred grid right, draggable divider), one green, small working type, many routes into the same practice.
2. **Fails:** value-only grading that stays silent on a miss, a 1.5 MB canvas grid that traps keyboard focus, brand green at 3.23:1 under text, no dark mode, no skip link.
3. **On a phone** its grid starts 1,260 px down and draws about 2.8 s after it is scrolled to.
4. **Intentional deviations here (brief and directions D, revisions 2–4):** `#18804F` for text, a server-rendered `<table>` grid, a verdict that explains every miss, dark theme, skip link, no third-party requests, one order everywhere, no streaks or sign-up banner. The owner lifted the bans on the split hero, a counts strip (real counts only), "How it works", cards with soft shadows, an optional stopwatch and the completion dialog (`docs/directions.md`, revisions 2 and 4).
5. **So** the split hero, counts strip, card shadows, timer and dialog are not defects here, although study section 11 lists them.
