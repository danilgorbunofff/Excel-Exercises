## Why employers test Excel

"Advanced Excel" on a CV can mean anything from SUM to a full financial model. A short test shows what a candidate can actually do, under time pressure, in a few minutes of the employer's time.

## The common formats

**Multiple choice.** Questions about what a formula returns, which function fits a task or what an error means. These test knowledge rather than speed.

**A simulated sheet.** An online test opens something that looks like Excel and asks you to enter formulas into given cells. Each answer is checked as you go or at the end.

**A take-home file.** You get a workbook with raw data and a list of tasks, often with 30 to 90 minutes to send it back. Someone opens the file and reads your formulas.

**A live task.** You share your screen in the interview and build something while you explain your thinking.

## Tasks that come up again and again

- Look up a value from another table, usually with XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP or INDEX and MATCH.
- Total or count by one or two conditions with SUMIFS and COUNTIFS.
- Flag rows with IF, often combined with AND or OR.
- Clean text: trim spaces, split a name, fix numbers stored as text.
- Work with dates: days between, month of a date, the last day of the month.
- Handle errors, so a missing match shows a message instead of `#N/A`.
- Build a small summary, such as sales by region, with formulas or a PivotTable.
- Find and fix the mistakes in a sheet someone else built.

## How they are marked

Most tests check the result in each cell. Many also check how you got it:

- A typed number where a formula belongs usually scores nothing.
- A formula that only works for the current data, such as a range that stops one row short, may be marked down.
- In take-home files, readable formulas, clear labels and a check total make a good impression.

## How to do well

- Read every task before you start, and do the quick ones first.
- Lock ranges with F4 before copying formulas.
- Check one or two answers by hand.
- If you get stuck, move on and come back.
- In a take-home, add a short note listing any assumptions.

## Practise under a timer

The best preparation is solving the same kinds of task against the clock. The [mock assessments](/mock-assessments/) on this site are timed sets marked at the end, like an employer's test.
