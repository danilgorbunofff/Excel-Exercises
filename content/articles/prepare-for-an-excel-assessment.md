## The approach

Two weeks is enough to get fast at the tasks that make up most Excel tests. The plan below takes about 45 minutes a day, and you build formulas every day.

Before day 1, try one timed [mock assessment](/mock-assessments/). Your score tells you where to spend extra time.

## Week 1: the core functions

**Day 1. References and basic maths.** Relative and absolute references, F4, SUM, AVERAGE, MIN and MAX.

**Day 2. IF and logic.** IF, AND, OR and IFS. Write tests with two or three conditions.

**Day 3. Counting and totals with conditions.** COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, SUMIF and SUMIFS, with text, numbers and dates as conditions.

**Day 4. Lookups.** XLOOKUP and VLOOKUP with an exact match. Then INDEX and MATCH.

**Day 5. Lookup problems.** `#N/A` and its causes: spaces, text numbers and missing values. IFNA and IFERROR.

**Day 6. Text.** TRIM, LEFT, MID, RIGHT, FIND, SUBSTITUTE and joining text with `&`.

**Day 7. Review.** Redo the tasks you got wrong this week, without looking at the answers first.

## Week 2: speed and combinations

**Day 8. Dates.** Days between dates, EOMONTH, EDATE and totals by month with SUMIFS.

**Day 9. Two-way lookups and multiple conditions.** INDEX with two MATCH functions, and lookups on two columns.

**Day 10. Summaries.** Build a small report: totals by region and month, with a check total. Try a PivotTable too.

**Day 11. Fixing sheets.** Take a sheet with broken formulas and find each problem.

**Day 12. A full timed test.** Work under the clock, then review every answer.

**Day 13. Weak spots.** Spend the day on whatever the timed test showed.

**Day 14. Light review.** One short timed set, then rest.

## Habits that pay off on the day

- Learn the shortcuts that save the most time: Ctrl+Arrow, Ctrl+Shift+Arrow, F4 and Ctrl+D.
- Always give lookups an exact match.
- Check one answer by hand in each task.
- Skip a stuck task and come back to it.

## Use spaced review

Redo a task a day or two after you first solve it, then again a few days later. The [Home page](/home/) shows which exercises are due for review, so you do not have to track it yourself.
